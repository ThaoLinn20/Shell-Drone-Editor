## Features
- Added automatic natural height variance across all firework and comet patterns to ensure burst altitudes feel organic and avoid repetitive flat lines.
- Added full multi-selection batch synchronization for Second Burst settings and sub-shell effects in the Property Inspector.
- Added custom launch angle support for the random pattern when specified by the user.
- Added a dedicated color picker for customizing Tadpole star head and particle flurry colors directly in the inspector.
- Enhanced water surface reflections and visual clarity for realistic lake and bay show environments.

## Updates
- Optimized sequence trajectory calculations and boundary safeguards for smoother multi-shell launches.
